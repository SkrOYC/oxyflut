## Options and trade-offs

- **Option A:** Use a scheduler-owned, per-window `IDXGISwapChain2::GetFrameLatencyWaitableObject` loop and a separate harness-owned `IDXGIOutput::WaitForVBlank` meter, with the active-mode, correlation, noninterference, and migration procedure below.
- **Option B:** Evaluate only `DCompositionWaitForCompositorClock` with `IDCompositionDevice::GetFrameStatistics`, or the Windows 11 Composition Swapchain `IPresentationManager` present-statistics queue, as acknowledgement alternatives.
- **Option C:** Retain timing, acknowledgement, recovery, mapping, source-fixture, host-installation, and variable-refresh rows as gating KUs when the required immutable capture, host approval, or controlled trace is absent.

The optional meter-only phase cannot satisfy an acceptance count. For each required stream in each output and phase, calculate count, minimum, p50, p95, p99, maximum, and its same-role consecutive interval distribution. For the scheduler stream, also calculate `sum(max(0, round(interval / T) - 1))` for intervals at least `1.5T` and `abs(mean(interval) - T) / T`. In the co-run only, greedily pair timestamp-ordered eligible scheduler opportunities and meter events one-to-one when their absolute QPC time difference is at most `T / 4`. Before calculating a derived statistic, fail the output if either required stream has fewer than `N` observations, any denominator is zero, fewer than two usable pairs exist, either interval distribution is empty, either paired timestamp variance is zero, or any derived statistic is nonfinite.
